Contacting Dassatek

When you submit the contact form, Google Forms collects the information you enter. Dassatek uses it to respond to your enquiry and discuss the interests or opportunities you describe. Contact us at dassatek@gmail.com to ask about your information or request deletion.

Google Forms

The embedded form is a Google service. Google may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details under its own privacy policy. Please do not submit passwords or sensitive personal information.

Video

Other links

Website analytics and hosting

This website does not include a dedicated analytics or advertising tracker. The hosting provider may process technical access logs needed to deliver and secure the website.
