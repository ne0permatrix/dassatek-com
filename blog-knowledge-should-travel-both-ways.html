Knowledge should travel both ways

Why we are connecting research, farmer experience, and Cambodian realities.

Dassatek · 3 October 2026

From a useful idea to a useful practice

An idea can be promising on paper and still raise many questions on a farm. What materials are available? How much time does it take? Does it fit the season, the soil, and the household? These are the questions we want our Knowledge Hub to help people explore.

Learning moves in both directions

Researchers bring methods and evidence. Farmers bring close observation, practical experience, and local knowledge. Dassatek’s vision is to create space for these perspectives to meet, with clear sources and accessible explanations.

A growing collection

The hub brings together selected resources on agroecology and agroforestry, alongside a framework for sharing local field learning. We will build the collection over time with practical guides, research summaries, and lessons from demonstration work.

Make knowledge relevant

For every resource, we want to ask what it can help a Cambodian farmer or learner understand, and what still needs local testing. You can explore the first resources now, or get in touch to suggest a useful source or research connection.
